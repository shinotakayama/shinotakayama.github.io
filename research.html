Research

My research lies at the intersection of microeconomic theory, political economy, and financial economics. I am particularly interested in how institutions shape strategic behaviour, collective decision-making, the aggregation of dispersed information, and economic outcomes. My work combines rigorous theoretical modelling with empirical and policy-oriented research.
